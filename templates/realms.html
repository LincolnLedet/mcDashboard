How it works

Each chunk is scored per player from CoreProtect's block log, surface activity only (mines and caves don't count):

blocks placed: chests, beds, furnaces and workstations count most; dirt, stone, crops, water and leaves don't count.
time spent: 5-minute stretches with any building, chest use, chat or command there.

Older activity fades by half every 3 weeks. Where two areas overlap, the stronger claim wins. Redrawn every 3 hours.

The map is the live BlueMap: drag to pan, right-drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Click a territory for its details.
